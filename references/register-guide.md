# Register Guide — Choosing How to Write Malay

Pick the register **before** writing. Most "machine Malay" comes from the wrong register (baku in a WhatsApp, `anda` to a spouse), not from grammar mistakes.

Sources: the register ladder and pronoun ladder in [basahan.md](basahan.md) and [baku.md](baku.md). Letter conventions are DBP PRPM Khidmat Nasihat rulings (cited in baku.md §11). UI defaults come from the i18nstack `localize-ms` skill (MIT). Everything else is native-speaker judgement [NK]: no published source maps situations to registers for Malaysian Malay.

## Register scale

| Level | Name | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Baku rasmi | Government letter, legal notice, formal report |
| R2 | Baku mesra | Office email, LinkedIn, CV, brand website, product UI |
| R3 | Santai sopan | WhatsApp to boss or colleague, customer DM, IG caption, family elders |
| R4 | Santai rapat | Friends, siblings, spouse |

## Choosing rules

1. **Mirror first.** If they wrote `I/you` and English, reply the same way. If they wrote `Assalamualaikum Encik`, reply at that level or one step above. Never go below the other person's level with someone senior.
2. **When unsure, go one step more polite**, never two. R3 is the safe default for any unknown Malaysian chat recipient. R2 is the default for any unknown email recipient.
3. **Hierarchy beats closeness in writing.** You might joke with your boss in person, but a written WhatsApp to them stays at R3 unless they write R4 first.
4. **Age:** to anyone clearly older, never `aku/kau` or `weh`. Use `abang/kakak/pakcik/makcik` or `saya` with their title.
5. **One pronoun pair per conversation.** Don't drift from `saya/awak` to `aku/kau` in the same message.
6. **`anda` is only for one-to-many text** (UI, ads, notices, forms). In one-to-one text it sounds like a bank or a robot.
7. **Religious openers:** for Muslim Malay recipients, `Assalamualaikum` / `Salam` opens most first messages to elders, bosses and strangers. Use `Salam sejahtera` / `Hai` / `Selamat pagi` for mixed or non-Muslim recipients.
8. **Customer service in Malaysia is warm, not formal.** `ye`, `sis`, an emoji and a quick answer beat `Terima kasih kerana menghubungi kami. Pertanyaan anda amat dihargai.`
9. **Code-switching follows the setting.** Office topics (meeting, deadline, slide) pull English in, even in Malay messages. Family, food, feelings and religion pull Malay in.
10. **Spelling follows register.** R1–R2: perfect baku. R3: full words plus `tak/dah/nak/je/tu/ni`. R4: whatever the person types (`x`, `yg`, `dh`, `2` reduplication).
11. **Complaints: calm it down first.** Open with an apology and the fix you're taking on, give a date, and offer a choice where you can. Never open by correcting the customer, defending yourself or quoting policy, even when the customer is at fault. State any fact about the customer's side neutrally (`courier tak jumpa nombor unit`), never as blame (`alamat cik tak lengkap`).

## Worked scenarios

**1. Customer DM, small online shop (R3).** Customer: `Sis, baju kurung moden ni ada saiz L lagi tak?`
- ✗ `Terima kasih kerana menghubungi kami. Untuk makluman anda, saiz L masih tersedia. Adakah anda ingin membuat tempahan?`
- ✓ `Hai sis! Saiz L ada lagi ye, tinggal 2 helai je. Nak saya hold dulu? 😊`
- Follow-up: `Dah terima payment, terima kasih sis! Esok pagi saya pos, nanti saya bagi tracking ye.`

**2. Late delivery complaint (R3, more careful).** Customer: `Dah seminggu tak sampai ni.`
- ✗ `Kami memohon maaf atas sebarang kesulitan yang dialami. Pihak kami akan menyiasat perkara ini dengan segera.`
- ✓ `Maaf sangat cik, saya dah check, parcel tersangkut kat hub Shah Alam. Saya dah follow up dengan courier, InsyaAllah sampai lusa. Kalau tak sampai juga, saya hantar ganti ye.`

**2a. Angry customer, scam accusation (R3, de-escalate).** Customer: `Dah 2 minggu tak sampai!! Korang ni scam ke hah? Saya report polis nanti.`
- ✗ `Bukan scam ye cik. Parcel dah dipos, cik boleh semak tracking sendiri.` (argues first, hands the work back)
- ✓ `Maaf sangat cik, dua minggu memang lama, saya faham cik risau. Saya dah check, parcel cik masih tersangkut kat hub Pos Laju Shah Alam, dan saya dah buat aduan dengan Pos Laju hari ni. Kalau tak sampai sebelum Jumaat, saya hantar ganti atau refund penuh, ikut mana cik nak. Esok pagi saya update cik ye 🙏`
- The action answers the accusation; the reply never says `bukan scam`.

**2b. Customer at fault, still no blame (R3, de-escalate).** The address had no unit number and the parcel came back. Customer: `Kenapa parcel saya return?? Courier korang bodoh ke?`
- ✗ `Untuk makluman, alamat yang cik berikan tidak lengkap.`
- ✓ `Maaf cik, parcel cik dipulangkan sebab courier tak jumpa nombor unit rumah. Saya nak pastikan kali ni sampai terus: boleh cik bagi alamat penuh dengan nombor unit? Esok saya pos semula.`
- The fact is stated about the courier's trip, not the customer's mistake. Who pays the second postage is the seller's call, not a language rule.

**3. Boss on WhatsApp (R3 careful).** Boss: `Report Q3 dah siap?`
- ✗ `Ya, laporan suku ketiga telah disiapkan dan akan dihantar sebentar lagi.` (too stiff)
- ✗ `Dah boss, jap lagi aku send.` (`aku` to a boss)
- ✓ `Dah Encik Hafiz, tinggal semak angka akhir. Saya hantar sebelum 5 petang ni.`

**4. Spouse (R4).** Wife: `Bang, nanti ambik Aisyah kat taska boleh? Saya meeting sampai 6.`
- ✗ `Baiklah, saya akan mengambil Aisyah dari taska.`
- ✓ `Boleh yang. Abang gerak pukul 5.30. Nak beli apa-apa untuk dinner?`

**5. Family group, news to elders (R3 warm).**
- ✓ `Assalamualaikum semua. Alhamdulillah, Aina dah selamat bersalin pagi tadi, baby lelaki 3.2kg. Dua-dua sihat. Mohon doakan ye.`

**6. Friends group (R4).**
- ✓ `Weh Sabtu ni futsal jadi tak? Aku dah book court 9 malam.` / Reply: `On. Bawak bola sekali la, bola aku pancit.`

**7. Brand IG caption (R3).**
- ✗ `Dalam era yang serba pantas ini, kami menawarkan pelbagai produk berkualiti tinggi yang pasti memenuhi citarasa anda.`
- ✓ `Restock dah sampai! Tudung bawal satin warna baru: dusty pink, sage, mocha. RM29 je, pos percuma Semenanjung.`

**8. Email to HR (R2).**
- ✓ `Salam Puan Rosmah,` / `Saya ingin memohon cuti kecemasan pada 26 November kerana anak saya demam. Sijil cuti sakit akan saya kemukakan selepas ini. Terima kasih atas pertimbangan puan.` / `Salam hormat,` / `Hafiz`

**9. CV bullet (R2).**
- ✗ `Saya adalah seorang yang bertanggungjawab dan mempunyai pengalaman yang luas dalam bidang pemasaran.`
- ✓ `Mengurus belanjawan pemasaran RM1.2 juta setahun.` / `Melancarkan 3 kempen digital yang meningkatkan pendaftaran pelanggan sebanyak 40%.`

## Quick self-check before sending

- Would this person use the same pronoun for themselves? If not, change it.
- Is there any `tidak`, `sahaja`, `sudah`, `tersebut`, `bagaimanapun`, `oleh itu` in an R3–R4 message? Replace each with its casual form.
- Is there any `x`, `yg`, `weh` or `la` in an R1–R2 text? Remove it.
- More than one particle per short sentence? Cut down.
- Any Indonesian word (`bisa`, `nggak`, `aja`, `gimana`, `banget`)? Replace (see malay-ai-tells.md).
